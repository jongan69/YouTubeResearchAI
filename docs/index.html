Capabilities

Built like a research assistant

🔬

Literature search

Queries arXiv, Semantic Scholar, CrossRef & OpenAlex in parallel. Results scored by citations, recency & relevance.

✅

Claim verification

Every claim checked against the literature — well-supported, plausible, contested, speculative, or opinion.

📝

Proper citations

APA, Chicago, or IEEE. Every reference includes a DOI. Hallucinated citations are pruned.

🎓

Domain-aware

CS, medicine, social sciences, humanities, natural sciences — auto-detected with a tailored strategy.

🔄

Iterative research

Multi-pass: finds gaps, runs targeted searches, and re-verifies until the findings converge.

🏗️

Provider-agnostic

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or any compatible endpoint. Degrades gracefully when a service is down.
